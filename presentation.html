PROBLEM STATEMENT

Challenges in Medical Claim Processing

⏳

Manual Bottlenecks & Delays

Insurance caseworkers spend 25–45 minutes per claim manually verifying hospital bills, line items, and diagnostic codes, leading to settlement delays of up to 30 days.

💸

High Operational Costs & Errors

Human coding errors in ICD-10/SNOMED CT mapping and missed policy exclusions account for over $14B annually in wasted administrative costs and overpayments.

⚠️

Fraud & Duplicate Billing Risks

Lack of real-time fingerprinting allows duplicate claims submitted across multiple clinics within short timeframes to bypass traditional audit filters.

🏛️

Government Scheme Friction

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) hospital claim verification involves complex 3-gate checks, leading to rejection of valid claims due to missing documentation.

PROPOSED SOLUTION

The MED-CLAIM Adjudication Platform

🧠

Autonomous 6-Gate Pipeline

Fully automated end-to-end processing pipeline evaluating OCR document intelligence, clinical coding, welfare eligibility, duplicates, and fraud guardrails in under 400ms.

👥

Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Queue

Intelligent triage automatically routes high-confidence claims (≥90%) to Instant Auto-Approval while routing ambiguous/handwritten bills to a caseworker review queue.

🇮🇳

PM-JAY Scheme Integration

Built-in Ayushman Bharat 3-gate checker, Aadhaar e-KYC card generator, and real-time family ₹5 Lakh annual cap tracking engine.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

6-Stage Processing Pipeline

  • Stage 1: Document OCR & IDP: Multi-engine Intelligent Document Processing for scanned bills, lab reports, and handwritten notes.
  • Stage 2: Structuring & Fingerprinting: Extracts patient metadata, facility names, line items, and matches provider signatures.
  • Stage 3: ICD-10 & SNOMED CT Coding: Automated clinical code mapping with confidence scoring and term harmonization.
  • Stage 4: Eligibility & Duplicate Check: Validates active policy coverage and checks 7-day twin-claim fingerprinting window.
  • Stage 5: Fraud Risk Scoring: Itemized pricing anomaly detection and risk scoring guardrails.
  • Stage 6: Government Portal Submission: Direct integration with NHA PM-JAY portal for instant disbursement.

ACCESSIBILITY & I18N

Multilingual & Mobile-First Design

🌐

10 Language Support

Full i18n support for 8 Indian regional languages (Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Marathi, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi) plus English and Spanish with instant DOM localization.

📱

Mobile Responsive Layout

Slide-over navigation drawer, touch-friendly 44px targets, horizontal table scroll containers, and responsive card grids for flawless operation on smartphones.

DOCUMENT INTELLIGENCE

Advanced OCR & Data Extraction

📄

Clean Hospital Bills

Extracts itemized billing tables, admission/discharge dates, room rates, and tax calculations with >98% accuracy for instant auto-approval.

🧪

Lab & Blood Reports

Parses blood parameters (CBC, LFT, Widal, ESR), reference ranges, and abnormal value flags to validate clinical necessity.

✍️

Messy Handwritten Notes

Detects low OCR confidence (<70%) or ambiguous doctor handwriting, automatically flagging claims for caseworker HITL review.

SECURITY & GUARDRAILS

Fraud Detection & Twin-Claim Prevention

🔍

7-Day Twin Claim Fingerprinting

Fingerprints patient identity, provider ID, and diagnostic codes to instantly block duplicate claims submitted across clinics within a 7-day window.

🛡️

Price Anomaly & Risk Scoring

Computes risk scores based on procedure price limits, unbundling detection, and provider history. Fraud score >0.60 triggers mandatory HITL audit.

GOVERNMENT SCHEME INTEGRATION

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) Portal

🏛️

3-Gate Eligibility Checker

Verifies patient identity, hospital empanelment status, and 1,929 procedure package rates in real-time.

🪪

Aadhaar e-KYC Card Generator

Simulates 12-digit Aadhaar OTP verification and generates downloadable PM-JAY Gold Ayushman Cards.

📊

Family ₹5 Lakh Cap Tracker

Monitors annual coverage balance, senior citizen separate caps, patient co-pay, and remaining family funds.
