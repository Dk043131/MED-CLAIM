    How to Submit a Real Bill

    Upload any real hospital bill, discharge summary, or medical invoice for AI-assisted adjudication.

    📷 Image / PDF (Recommended)
    Upload a photo or scan of your hospital bill (JPG, PNG, PDF, WEBP). The AI backend reads the document via OCR and extracts all patient, hospital and billing data automatically.
    ⚡ Requires backend connection for OCR.
    📄 Text File (.txt)
    Works offline. Paste bill content as a .txt file with Patient Name, Hospital, diagnosis, and amounts (₹/Rs./INR). The system parses it directly in the browser.
    ℹ️ After upload, your claim enters the HITL Review Queue for human verification before PM-JAY portal submission. No claim is auto-approved.
